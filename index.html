FAQ

Questions worth answering upfront.

What does a typical engagement look like?

Every engagement runs the same three stages: assess what's running today, build to a defined standard, then stay through stabilization and train your team whether it's a one-week audit or a multi-month build.

Do you work alongside our existing team, or fully take over?

Both. Engagements can be scoped as embedded support alongside your engineers, or as a fully managed build-and-run mandate whatever fits how your team already operates.

Which cloud providers and platforms do you support?

AWS, Microsoft Azure, VMware, and Kubernetes, plus on-prem and hybrid environments. See the full list of supported platforms and tooling above.

Do you only do cloud work, or on-prem as well?

Both. Many engagements are hybrid by design some workloads staying on-prem for cost, compliance, or latency reasons, others migrating to cloud.

How is training delivered?

Hands-on sessions built around your actual stack, not generic slides delivered remotely or on-site, depending on what works for your team.

How fast do you respond to new enquiries?

Within 1 business day.

Have a system that needs building — or fixing?

Tell us what you're running and where it's headed. We'll reply with a straight read on scope, not a sales pitch.
